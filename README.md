## Academic chronology and provenance

The archive owner confirmed on **15 October 2026** that fourth year has just begun and **all currently archived coursework was completed through third year**. Thus the 4xx-coded ACSC430 and ACSC476 coursework is now filed under **Study Year 3**, not Year 4. This is the latest eligible study-year grouping, not independent evidence of the exact year either course was taken. Lower-code courses (1xx, 2xx, 3xx) keep their provisional Years 1–3 classification. No folder for fourth-year coursework is present until actual fourth-year work is added.

The semester rule is unchanged: the final two digits **below 50** indicate Semester 1, **above 50** Semester 2, and exactly **50** is unresolved. This remains a user-supplied heuristic, not confirmation of the official university curriculum. **ACSC131** was once archived in Semester 2 based on historical folder evidence; the revised Semester 1 classification follows the heuristic. The original paths and classification evidence are preserved in `MIGRATION_MAP.json`.

Uncoded courses remain under `Academic-Year-Uncertain/Semester-Uncertain`. Calendar academic years are still not established by the available reports. Backup timestamps and publication dates are not academic chronology evidence. NewVOL was a Windows-origin backup inspected on a Mac. Arduino remains outside the repository because no Frederick course association was established.
